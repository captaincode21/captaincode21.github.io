Published conference paper

Proactive Prediction of Gas Hydrate Formation in Subsea Pipelines Using Continuous-Time Data-Driven Modelling

O. S. Bisiriyu, O. I. Oyelumade, O. D. Oyeyemi and P. O. Akomolafe. SPE Nigeria Annual International Conference and Exhibition (NAICE), Paper ID 380, August 2026.

A Neural ODE predicts the hydrate mass in the oil layer at the topside of a subsea pipeline from 16 process variables recorded at three nodes. It used nine OLGA FA simulation cases that varied pressure (180 to 220 bar), temperature (40 to 60 °C) and total water fraction (0.30 to 0.50).

Undergraduate thesis

Data-Driven Nonlinear Model Predictive Control Using a Stability-Constrained Latent Neural Ordinary Differential Equation

Obafemi Awolowo University, 2026. Supervisor: Dr. Ayorinde Bamimore.

A latent neural ODE with an LSTM encoder, a latent vector field that is contractive by construction and a decoder, trained from measurable inputs and outputs only. The guarantee is incremental input-to-state stability of the latent model.

The model was used for prediction in an offset-free NMPC with a disturbance observer, tested in simulation on a pH neutralisation process, a nonlinear CSTR with an unstable steady state and a cascaded three-tank system.
